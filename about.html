Welcome to Vivid Study SA!

We know how stressful school can be. Whether you are trying to master tricky maths equations, memorise science definitions, or find quick study notes before a big test, schoolwork is a lot to handle. That is why Vivid Study SA was created: to make studying simpler, faster, and completely free for South African students from primary school up to Grade 10.

What We Offer

Clear Study Guides: easy-to-read summaries broken down by grade and subject.

Free Printable PDFs: worksheets, cheat sheets and practice material you can download and print anytime.

Practice and Revision: tools and tests to check your understanding before exams (coming soon).

YouTube Video Lessons: step-by-step video explanations linked from our channel (coming soon).

Our mission is to help students study smarter, feel more confident, and do well in their exams without the stress. Grab your notes, watch our lessons, and let's make studying easier together!
